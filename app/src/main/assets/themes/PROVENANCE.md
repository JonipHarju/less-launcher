# Theme wallpaper provenance

All bundled artwork is from the National Gallery of Art Open Access programme
and is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The two non-artwork Themes ship generated assets, not flat colour fills.

to a phone portrait without altering colour. No Theme asset is fetched at runtime.
