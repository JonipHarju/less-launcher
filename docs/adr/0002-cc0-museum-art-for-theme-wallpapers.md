# Theme wallpapers come from CC0 museum Open Access collections

Bundled wallpapers are sourced from public-domain museum Open Access programmes
(the Met, the Smithsonian, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Rijksmuseum, the
National Gallery of Art), which
release high-resolution images under CC0, and each carries a Credit naming its
artist and collection.

## Considered Options

**Unsplash / Pexels** — their licences permit commercial use, but Unsplash's
forbids "compiling images to replicate a similar or competing service" and does
not clearly address redistributing originals inside a shipped artifact. An
Apache-2.0 public repo containing the original files sits in that grey zone.

**AI-generated images** — rejected on preference: the project would rather show
work by human artists and name them.

**Living artists asked directly** — the nicest option and the slowest. Left open
for a future set.

CC0 is unambiguous for redistribution inside an open-source repo, and crediting
the artist becomes a feature of the Theme picker rather than a licence
obligation. The two non-art Themes ship generated near-black and off-white
assets instead, so that the plainest Themes still render identically on every
device.
